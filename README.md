# 🎂 Happy Birthday Payel! ✨

A handcrafted, interactive birthday web experience created with love for Payel's special day.

## 🌸 Features & Interactive Journey

1. **Vintage Wax-Seal Envelope**:
   - Cottagecore watercolor aesthetics with painted tulips, delicate hearts, and gentle floating rose petals.
   - Realistic 3D opening animation triggered by breaking the deep crimson wax seal.

2. **Make a Wish (Candle Scene)**:
   - Ambient warm flickering candle with realistic glow and rising smoke wisp.
   - Tap or blow interaction to extinguish the candle.

3. **Lights Out & Switch On**:
   - Playful blackout transition with a glowing switch to reveal the celebration.

4. **Interactive Touch Cake Cutting**:
   - Tap anywhere on the cake to slice it, revealing a fresh strawberry slice with a cute teddy bear accent.

5. **Celebration Party**:
   - Full-clarity party celebration video frame with music and joyful confetti.

6. **Dedicated Birthday Video (`vdo2.mp4`)**:
   - Full-screen high-clarity portrait video of Payel accompanied by heartfelt birthday wishes.

7. **Photo Album & Memories Feed**:
   - Smooth continuous vertical scroll of cherished photo memories (`pic1` through `pic9`) with thoughtful captions.
   - Interactive **"Send Love"** heart counter with burst animations and confetti effects.
   - Replay button to experience the magic all over again.

## 💖 Tech Stack

- **HTML5** & **Semantic Audio/Video API**
- **Modern CSS3** (Flexbox, CSS Grid, 3D transforms, keyframe animations, glassmorphism)
- **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)** with procedural confetti canvas and Web Audio synthesizer
- Fully responsive design optimized for mobile and desktop screens
